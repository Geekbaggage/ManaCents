# Paste-into-Gemini prompt: the ManaCents theme song (fixed by Robin, Oct 20, 2026)

---

Create an original **instrumental** theme for ManaCents — **no vocals, no lyrics, no
spoken words.**

**What ManaCents is:** a Magic: The Gathering Commander tool that builds budget-friendly
upgrade lists for preconstructed decks. You pick a commander, you name your budget, it
brews the list. The brand feeling is *clever beats expensive* — this is the sound of a
$0.30 card taking over the game. Confident, a little mischievous, never corporate,
never try-hard epic.

**Length:** 30–35 seconds, never over 40. It must **loop seamlessly** — the ending
flows back into the beginning.

**Style:** dark futuristic synthwave blended with cyberpunk electronic, light cinematic
polish, modern game-soundtrack production.

**Mood:** mysterious, clever, futuristic, confident, quietly epic — like discovering a
powerful strategy nobody else saw, without spending a fortune. Add a subtle
multiverse / reality-fracture shimmer to the textures, but keep it neutral enough to
fit any Magic set, commander, or archetype.

**Sound design:** deep but controlled synth bass (audible on headphones, never boomy
on phone speakers), one memorable pulsing synth melody, crisp electronic percussion,
subtle digital glitches, airy atmospheric pads, and a small cinematic rise into a
satisfying beat.

**Signature motif:** invent a distinctive 3–5 note melodic motif that works as the
ManaCents audio logo. State it in the **first 3 seconds** and bring it back at the
**end**, so listeners recognize it instantly across every video.

**Arc:** (1) the motif hook, immediately; (2) a catchy synth groove with steady
percussion; (3) bass and subtle layers build momentum while staying
background-friendly; (4) a satisfying melodic resolution that loops into the start.

**Mix:** professional, clean, polished. This plays *under* slideshow transitions, card
images, decklists, budget numbers, and on-screen text — it must support the content,
never fight it. Avoid: vocals, aggressive drops, excessive distortion, busy melodies,
dramatic orchestral swells.
