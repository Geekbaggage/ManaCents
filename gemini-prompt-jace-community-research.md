# Paste-into-Gemini prompt: community research for the Jace budget upgrade

---

Research task: find what the Magic community actually recommends for upgrading the
**Multiverse Reforged** Commander precon on a budget, and bring me a structured,
sourced report. This feeds a real product (ManaCents) and a social post, so accuracy
matters more than volume — if you can't verify something, say so instead of filling
the gap.

## The deck
- Commander: **Jace, Multiverse Architect** (Legendary Planeswalker, four colors
  W/U/B/R — no green), from *Reality Fracture Commander*, released October 2, 2026.
  Precon name: **Multiverse Reforged** ($49.99 premium precon).
- His −3 ability is a polymorph effect: exile a creature or planeswalker you control,
  then reveal cards from the top of your library until you hit a creature or
  planeswalker card and put it onto the battlefield.
- The stock deck's plan: make tokens (Elspeth, Martial Coup, Secure the Wastes), then
  swap them for haymakers (Archon of Cruelty, Serra's Emissary, Darksteel Angel) with
  Jace's −3, Mass Polymorph, Synthetic Destiny, and Proteus Staff.

## Sources to check (post-release content only — Oct 2, 2026 or later)
- **Reddit:** r/BudgetBrews, r/EDH, r/magicTCG — threads on Multiverse Reforged
  upgrades, Jace Multiverse Architect builds, budget adds/cuts.
- **Facebook:** MTG Commander / EDH / budget-brewing groups and the TCGplayer
  Facebook page (posts and comments about this precon's upgrades).
- If a source is closed or you can't actually access it, name it and mark it
  "not checked" — do NOT summarize what you imagine is in there.

## Hard rules for every recommendation
1. **The Polymorph Trap:** never recommend small utility creatures (mana dorks,
   2-mana value creatures, etc.). Flipping into one off Jace's −3 bricks the ability.
   Every creature/planeswalker recommended must be a haymaker worth cheating in.
   Good categories: (a) non-creature spells that make tokens, (b) top-of-library
   manipulation, (c) proliferate / planeswalker protection / mana rocks,
   (d) big haymaker creatures or planeswalkers.
2. **Nothing already in the precon.** Pull the official Wizards decklist for
   Multiverse Reforged and exclude all 99 cards from your recommendations.
3. **Color identity WUBR only** — no green, no colorless-only assumptions that break
   the deck's plan (colorless cards are legal if they fit).
4. **Prices:** approximate current TCGplayer low / Scryfall USD, with today's date
   attached. Flag any card whose price is spiking.

## What to bring back
1. **Tier 1 — the sub-$1 adds:** the 10–15 best upgrades under $1 each, ranked by
   community consensus. For each: how many independent sources recommended it,
   its category (a–d above), one line on why it works here, and what stock card it
   replaces.
2. **Tier 2 — the full ~$40 upgrade:** the adds + cuts package the community
   converges on for about $40 total.
3. **The cuts list:** stock cards the community most agrees should come OUT, and why.
4. **Anti-recommendations:** popular-sounding cards people warn AGAINST for this
   deck (especially anything that falls into the Polymorph Trap).
5. **The combo question:** the stock list contains an infinite combo (Brainstorm +
   Dack Fayden + Helping Hand + Venser, Fervent Forger). Is the community building
   around it, ignoring it, or cutting pieces of it?
6. **Disagreements:** where Reddit and Facebook (or threads within them) conflict,
   show both sides instead of picking a winner.
7. **Citations:** every pick names its source(s) — subreddit or group/page name plus
   the thread/post. Anything backed by only one source gets marked "single-source."

Format: clean markdown, tables where they help. No fluff intro — lead with Tier 1.
