## The loop
1. Control Rooftop Storm, Diregraf Captain, Viscera Seer. Cast Gravecrawler for {0} — from hand or graveyard (Rooftop Storm covers Zombie spells cast from anywhere).
2. Sacrifice Gravecrawler to Viscera Seer: scry 1. It dies → Diregraf Captain triggers → target opponent loses 1 life.
3. Cast Gravecrawler from graveyard for {0} again — you control a Zombie (the Captain).
4. Repeat. Infinite scry, infinite life drain.

## Honest caveats
- Needs all four pieces on the battlefield, with Gravecrawler in hand or graveyard to start.
- Removal on any piece in response stops the loop cold (kill the Storm/Captain/Seer).
- Graveyard hate (Rest in Peace, etc.) ends the recast.
- Sorcery-speed setup; no haste needed (not a combat finish).
